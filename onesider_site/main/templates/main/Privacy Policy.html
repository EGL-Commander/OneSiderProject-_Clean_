Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information OneSider collects, why it is used, and how it is handled when you use the platform.

Last updated: August 2026

01. Information We Collect

When you create or use an account on OneSider, information such as your username, email address, and account-related information may be stored. Information associated with purchases, saved projects, and downloads may also be recorded to provide the corresponding functionality.

02. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and maintain your account, provide access to purchased assets, process platform functionality, maintain security, respond to support requests, and improve the OneSider experience.

03. Payment Information

Payments are handled through the payment provider used by OneSider. OneSider does not need to store your complete payment-card credentials in order to provide access to purchased digital assets.

04. Cookies & Local Storage

OneSider may use cookies and browser storage to maintain authentication, remember preferences, support site functionality, and provide a consistent experience.

05. Account Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect account and platform information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

06. Data Sharing

Personal information is not intentionally sold or rented to third parties. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate essential services, such as authentication, hosting, payment processing, or security.

07. Purchase Records

Records relating to successful purchases may be retained to provide access to purchased assets, maintain transaction history, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and verify legitimate transactions.

08. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate accounts, provide purchased services, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve legitimate disputes.

09. Your Choices

You may review and update certain account information through the available account settings. If you have a privacy-related request or concern, you may contact the OneSider administrator.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version published on this page will describe how information is handled at that time.

11. Contact

For questions or requests concerning privacy and personal information, please contact the OneSider administrator through the available contact channel.
