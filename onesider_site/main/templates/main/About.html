About

Not a team. Not a studio. One person, working alone, in silence.

01. The Whole Thing

This entire site, and every single project listed inside it, was built by me. Alone. The design, the backend, the payment flow, the download system, every project on the shelf — one person, no team, no co-founder, no agency behind the curtain. OneSider is not a storefront I set up for other people's work. It's mine, end to end.

02. Why "OneSider"

Built for those who move alone — that's not a tagline I borrowed, it's how this got made. No standups, no committee decisions on what shade of violet the accent color should be. One person deciding everything, and living with every consequence of that. If something here is sharp, that's why. If something here is still rough, that's why too.

03. What I Actually Used

I'm not going to pretend I did this in total isolation — that would be a different kind of dishonest. Claude and ChatGPT sat with me through a good chunk of the build: architecture decisions, debugging sessions, the payment hardening, the UI polish passes. I directed every one of those sessions, made every final call, and wrote or rewrote what needed my hand on it. Credit where it's due, on both sides.

04. What's On Here

Every project in the gallery is something I actually built and actually use as a reference for what "finished" looks like — not a placeholder, not a mockup padded out to look like a catalog. If it's listed, I stand behind it.

05. Get In Touch

Questions about a project, a purchase, or anything else — the Contact page goes straight to me. No support team in between.
